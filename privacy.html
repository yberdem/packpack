Privacy Policy

PackPack · Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Türkçe

PackPack is a single-player puzzle game: draw routes, deliver parcels and build your town. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and who processes it.

In short: there is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location permission. Your game progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads (Google AdMob), offers optional in-app purchases (handled by Apple / Google) and uses Firebase to count anonymous usage and to receive crash reports.

Data controller

Yüksel Berkay Erdem (Türkiye), the developer of PackPack, is the controller of the data described here. For any privacy question or request: support@league-racer.com.

Data that stays on your device

Your levels, coins, lives, boosters, town progress, streak and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are stored in the app's local storage on your device. We do not run a game server and these are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

PackPack shows two kinds of ads, served by Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward (for example extra coins, a life or a continue). Nothing is given or taken away if you don't watch.
  • Full-screen ads between levels, only after you win a level, from level 20 on, at most once every three levels and never during a level or after a loss. Buying "Remove Ads" or any other purchase turns them off.

To run ads Google may process your device's advertising identifier, a coarse location derived from your IP address (country/city), ad interactions and diagnostic data of the ad component. On iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in Apple's "Allow apps to track" prompt; if you don't, ads are still shown but not personalised with that identifier. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland you see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy options. Without consent you can still play the whole game.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics:

DataWhy
App-instance ID (a random ID created by Firebase, not linked to you)To count players and sessions without knowing who you are.
Game events: level started / won / lost, booster used, ad watched, purchase completed, with level number, app version, device model, OS version and languageTo balance difficulty and the in-game economy.
Crash reports: stack trace, app version, device model, OS versionTo find and fix crashes.

We don't use this data for advertising and don't sell it. Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs, the starter pack and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details; the app only receives a confirmation from the store. You can restore Remove Ads with Profile → Restore purchases.

Data we don't collect

  • Name, email, phone number, address
  • Precise location (we never ask for location permission)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone
  • Payment card details

Legal bases

Under the GDPR (Art. 6) and Türkiye's KVKK (Art. 5): consent for personalised ads and tracking; legitimate interests for crash reports, anonymous usage statistics and non-personalised ads that keep the game free; performance of a contract for purchases.

Children

PackPack is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

PackPack has no account, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. To delete your data:

  • Game progress (levels, coins, town, settings) is only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it at once and for good.
  • Analytics and crash data (Firebase) are tied to a random app-instance ID, not to you. Firebase deletes analytics after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days. To ask for earlier deletion, write to support@league-racer.com with the subject "Delete my data", your device model, the app version (bottom of Profile) and roughly when you played. We delete whatever we can match within 30 days and tell you the result.
  • Advertising data is held by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: turn off "Allow apps to request to track").
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google as required by law; we keep none.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of data related to you by writing to support@league-racer.com. Because we don't know who you are, tell us your device model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Profile) and roughly when you played; without an account we may not be able to single out your analytics data. You can also stop all collection by deleting the app. You may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: KVKK).

Changes

If this policy changes we update this page and the date at the top.
