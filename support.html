PackPack Support

Help · Yardım

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@league-racer.com and include your device model and the app version (bottom of the Profile page). We usually answer within two working days.

Soru, hata ya da önerin mi var? support@league-racer.com adresine cihaz modelini ve uygulama sürümünü (Profil sayfasının altında) yazarak ulaşabilirsin.

FAQ

  • My progress is gone. Progress is stored on the device only; deleting the app deletes it.
  • I bought Remove Ads on another device. Profile → Restore purchases.
  • How do I change ad consent? Profile → Privacy options.
  • How do I change the language? Profile → Language.
